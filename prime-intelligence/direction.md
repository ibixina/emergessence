# Prime Intelligence

The user supplied Antony Gormley, M. C. Escher, and the film Iron Man as visual references.

This edition interprets those references through an original spatial figure, a view-dependent architectural illusion, and precise tools that operate on the sculpture. No artist's image, sculpture mesh, film frame, character model, or trademark is included.

Reference reading:

- [Antony Gormley: Open Blockworks](https://www.antonygormley.com/works/sculpture/series/open-blockworks) — the relationship between body, architectural cells, solid mass, and open space.
- [M. C. Escher: Recognition & Success gallery](https://mcescher.com/gallery/recognition-success/) — spatial contradictions and the viewer's frame of reference.
- Iron Man is interpreted as a feeling of responsive engineering: disassembly, inspection, reassembly, and purposeful interaction.

The figure is generated from simple body volumes, sampled and fractured into geometric pieces. It is an abstract human form, not Shishir's likeness or an anatomical model. The same pieces become three ascending flights of stairs. The staircase's endpoints are displaced along the camera's viewing direction, making them coincide in projection at one calibrated view. Orbiting reveals that they do not physically meet. The traveling trace follows the actual flights; its wrap is continuous only in that projection.

The portfolio content remains live HTML. The memory and flock studies run actual small computational models. The sculpture and its lighting are expressive devices, not measured representations of neural activity.
